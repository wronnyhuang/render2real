- **Paired data from the de-render pipeline scales.** 138 frame-aligned 8-second pairs (render | real) from Meta Seamless Interaction in about 50 minutes on the host CPU: 41 adults, 126 train clips, 12 held-out clips from 8 held-out people. Real-vs-render mouth correlation is 0.95 median with zero lag, so the pairs are good supervision.
- **One-step SD-Turbo + LoRA (pix2pix-turbo style), 72 minutes of training on one RTX 5090.** Sharp faces, correct eyelids and mouth shapes, clothing colour and framing kept. Blind judge 24/24 over the render on held-out people; 16/16 on the Muse/Juno clips (realism 5.8 there).
- **Speed, measured end to end at 384 px, batch 1, RTX 5090:** 21.3 ms per frame (47 fps) with fp16 + torch.compile; 43.7 ms (23 fps) without compile. The denoiser is 9.5 ms; the VAE is most of the rest.
- **A free flicker fix:** blending 35% of the previous frame's latent brings frame-to-frame change from 1.21× real video to 0.97×, with lip correlation unchanged (0.91) and judge realism unchanged (8.0).
- **Wan 2.1 VACE 1.3B LoRA (504 steps, rank 32) with a CausVid 6-step sampler:** judge realism 7.7–8.1 on held-out clips, the steadiest identity of any variant (0.94–0.95, equal to real video), and with a prompt it keeps the render's hair, age and clothes.
- **The tiny conv U-Net (20M parameters) is the speed anchor:** 2.7 ms per frame (370 fps) and it tracks lips as well as the big model (0.93), so the motion signal is easy to carry; realism is the hard part.
